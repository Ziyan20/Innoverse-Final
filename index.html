Our Philosophy

A fragrance is the most intimate form of self-expression. It exists between moments — in the air after you leave a room, in a memory that returns without warning, in the quiet hour before dawn. We bottle that.

Heritage

Born from the
Heart of the Amazon

In 1994, Henri Leclerc ventured deep into the Amazon basin — not for commerce, but for curiosity. What he found changed fragrance forever: wild orchids thriving in volcanic soil, ancient resins weeping gold, botanicals unknown to Western noses.

Today, every Amazon Perfumes bottle carries that spirit of discovery. Our Paris atelier blends these extraordinary ingredients with three decades of olfactory mastery, producing scents not merely worn — but experienced.

H
Henri Leclerc
Founder & Master Perfumer · Paris
